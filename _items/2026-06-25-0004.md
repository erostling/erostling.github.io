shortdesc: "This postcard was inspired by the World War II poster, Rosie the Riveter, the iconic symbol of women working together for the greater good. In need of a single spool of thread, I’d planned a quick in-and-out visit to Hobby Lobby.
Once there, I was shocked and angered by the large percentage of shoppers and workers, most of whom were women, not wearing masks. I left immediately and drove half an hour to Greensboro for one spool of thread, not in fear of [COVID-19] but in anger over the selfishness I’d witnessed.
The simple act of wearing a mask had become a political statement. It was a way to say: It’s a free country and I am free to do what I want.
Eleanor Roosevelt told us, “with freedom comes responsibility.” Every generation has its opportunity to contribute to the wellbeing of the world, be it working on a factory line or wearing a mask.
This postcard honors all those who contribute to the greater good through simple day-to-day decisions and actions."
