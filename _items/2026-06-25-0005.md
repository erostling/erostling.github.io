shortdesc: "The day was warm and the breeze was brisk. The whirligigs clanged loudly, their shiny metal reflecting the light of a blinding sun. Jeff and I were reeling from the recent deaths of two treasured women in our lives, both known for spreading joy and kindness. Our trip to Wilson NC’s Whirligig Park that day was our first small step towards healing.
Inspired by that glimmer of happiness cracking the hard shell of grief surrounding me, I started this piece as a tribute to Laura and Grace. I posted it as part of my 100 Day Project with the caption, “Unable to attend the gathering in honor of my dear friend Laura on Friday, I kept thinking how [my day with these happy whirligigs] was symbolic of the joy and laughter she spread in life!” Five years later, I continue to feel transformed by the spirit of her friendship."
