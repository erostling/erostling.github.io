shortdesc: "I started [Iona’s Covid quilt] the first week of social distancing, knowing it would help keep me sane, but also wanting a tangible record of this time in our world and in our home. I used a torn bedsheet, some memory filled scraps, and a selection of hospital scrubs that I had picked up at Durham Rescue Mission for their color but had to put in this quilt as tribute to our health care heroes. It’s the softest quilt I’ve ever made, and I can only hope it provides Iona with as much comfort as she’s been providing me!”
