shortdesc: "I had good intentions with this diary quilt project, but after 5 months, living in a pandemic world caught up with my creative energy. I kept detailed records of new cases and deaths for several months, but some time during the online teaching, the summer, and the election, I just had to stop. The stories of loved ones dying alone, the lackluster and ignorant government response, and the lack of resources were too heartbreaking. Fortunately, I never lost anyone from the disease. There was nothing creative happening—just lots of anger and anxiety.
I signed up for the first vaccines as soon as I could and have had one every six months since Fall 2020. I have had COVID twice—both very mild cases. I made homemade masks for my students, and we shared COVID stories online during our shortened spring semester 2020, but we returned to the classroom very different people in in a different world in the fall. I returned to London with students in January 2021, and we had to test coming, going, and during—and wear masks. I used antibiotic wipes on groceries for months at my house and fully supported online shopping!
In September 2025, I received my most recent COVID shot (thanks to Gov. Stein!) without having to have a prescription—another way, our government is putting up health impediments. I feel like a warrior in this latest anti-vax period. Trump is once again in office, and I fear the consequences of another pandemic. I am 72 years old and have survived.
Statistics on this diary quilt come from the Centers for Disease Control website, CNN, the New York Times, and the Wall Street Journal. The opinions are mine."
