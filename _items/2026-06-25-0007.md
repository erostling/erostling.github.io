shortdesc: "The materiality of the pink satin helped me cope by demanding my senses. The color was happy, unapologetic, and the texture was smooth and cool to the touch. Sewing was reassurance that I existed in the physical world when so much of my pandemic experience was digital. I wanted to touch things other than my computer keys and iPhone screen. The dress screams “Look at me!” after months of feeling invisible, my headshot immortalized in a pixelized rectangle on Google Meet.
Junior Prom Dress is about hope. I was really convinced that in the time it took to complete, that prom would be in-person and normal. Sewing during the pandemic allowed me to take control of something and to see growth and progress in the physical space in front of me. The fabric could be touched, manipulated, and built into a secure garment when it felt like my world was falling apart."
